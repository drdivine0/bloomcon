One day, one connected journey

Everyone starts together in the Main Hall. In the afternoon you choose the track that fits what you need. The day closes with funding awarded live on stage.

  1. 1The Foundation

    Morning, for everyone

    Bloom Stage sets the purpose, The Playbook sharpens how you perform, and WellX looks after the person doing the work.

  2. 2The Pathway

    Afternoon, your choice

    Pick one: JobFest if you need a job, Globaverse to work smarter with AI, or BloomConnect if your business is ready for funding.

  3. 3The Proof

    Close, together

    The Bloom Fund recognises and funds ten accelerator graduates in front of the whole hall.
